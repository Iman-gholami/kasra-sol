# Kasra Palette B — high-resolution demonstration imagery

This Preview replaces the six **3 KB** experimental AVIF photo placeholders with 6 explicitly sourced, high-resolution fashion references from Unsplash. They are linked through the Next.js Image optimizer and are **not** product photographs, verified SKU colors, or evidence of Kasra's real inventory. These remote photos are used **only** by the demo product data in `src/data/products.ts`; backend-uploaded and real product photos remain untouched.

Replace the images with Kasra-owned, suitably model-released professional merchandise photos before accepting customer orders. Photo styles are references rather than a controlled shoot; source backgrounds may differ from the exact UI gray and are NOT color-keyed.

The six old low-resolution AVIF originals remain in `public/images/lookbook` for rollback. No destructive pixel transformations, AI upscaling, mask filters, extra client-side JS or build-time photo recompression are used.
